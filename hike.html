Settings

ETA uses Tobler's hiking function (slower uphill and steep downhill) from your base pace, then adjusts to your real pace once you've been walking 20 minutes — breaks included, so it's the arrival time you'd actually get.

Battery: position is only read while this app is on screen. Lock the phone or switch app and GPS stops; it resumes where you are when you come back. "Keep screen on" (⋯ menu) is off by default.
